Towards the end of 2022 coming off a [global pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19), I was very much in need of ways to get connected to my local community. I'd moved to Charlotte 2 years prior but had few opportunities to get out and about seeing as we were all focused on not dying at the time (social distancing, masks, fear, misinformation, loss, what a time COVID-19 was).

Once the world opened back up, I was determined to find reasons to be out of the house. I started with working remotely from [third places](https://www.charlottethirdplaces.com/) across the city, and continued with volunteerism, which let me both do good and meet new people in what still was an unfamiliar city.

One of the first organizations I volunteered with was the [Carolina Youth Coalition](https://carolinayouth.org/), starting online during COVID as a writing tutor for _[high-achieving underresourced students](https://carolinayouth.org/about/)_ working on college scholarship and application essays. That was great, but once in-person meetings kicked up again, I signed up to expand my volunteering into being a mentor, now reviewing essays and providing what guidance I could live in the flesh.

It's been 4 years now of volunteering with anything and everything related to CYC, and I recently got a chance to talk with their editor and share more about my story and reasons for volunteering.
